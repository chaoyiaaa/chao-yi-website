Our Story

Culture in Motion,
Legacy in Every Step

Chao Yi Athletic Association (澳洲潮義龍獅體育會) was founded by members of Brisbane's community to enhance and expand the culture of Lion Dance in Australia through performances, cultural celebrations, community services and to hand this culture forward, generation after generation, stronger and more united than we received it.

What We Value

  • IntegrityWe do what is right, even when no one is watching, in training, in leadership, and in how we treat one another.
  • RespectEarned through action, not assumed through title. Every member's voice matters.
  • DisciplineIn the way we train, perform, and carry ourselves as a team and as individuals.
  • Spiritual & Cultural AcknowledgementWe honour the traditions we carry. This is culture, not religion.

Get Involved

Join the Chao Yi Team

Are you ready to learn about the art of lion dance? You will not only learn to become a great lion dancer, but learn life-long skills like discipline, respect and determination. Meet new people, make new friends and join our family!

Lion Dance

Learn traditional lion dance stances, choreography and expression — from foundation to full performance.

Dragon Dance

Join a coordinated team carrying our dragon through sweeping, synchronized routines — great for teamwork and fitness.

Drumming & Cymbals

Master the rhythms that drive every performance — Chinese drum, gong and cymbal training for all skill levels.

Ready to train with us?

No experience needed — we train performers of all ages and backgrounds, from first-timers to competition-level athletes.
